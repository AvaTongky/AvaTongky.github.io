Projects

Do Audit Red Flags Reveal Earnings Management and Hard-to-Forecast Earnings?

Rdplyrggplot2WRDS

Group project for ACCT 3112. We tested whether internal control material weaknesses and restatements are linked to higher discretionary accruals and larger analyst forecast errors for U.S. firms, fiscal 2016 to 2025, using OLS regressions with year and industry controls on WRDS data.

Finding: Material weaknesses are linked to higher discretionary accruals and much larger analyst forecast errors, while restatements are linked to higher accruals but not to larger forecast errors. These are associations, not causal effects.
